A nurse practitioner’s walkthrough of how to actually take care of your skin — from morning routine to professional treatments — without the noise of trends.

By Imani Bryan · DNP, FNP-C, PMHNP-BC

The 4-Step Divine Routine

Five minutes morning, five minutes evening. The actual products change with the season; the framework stays the same.

1. Cleanse

Gentle, low-pH cleanser. No squeak, no foam burn. If you wore SPF or makeup, double cleanse: oil first, then gel.

2. Treat

Active layer chosen for your goal: vitamin C in the morning for tone; retinoid at night for cell turnover.

3. Moisturize

A barrier-supportive moisturizer with ceramides and a touch of hyaluronic. Never skip — even oily skin needs hydration.

4. SPF (AM)

Mineral or hybrid SPF 30+. The single best anti-aging product you’ll ever own. Reapply every 2 hours outdoors.

How to read your skin this week

Dull or tired

Increase hydration and exfoliation. Add a glow facial or IV hydration. Reduce alcohol and sleep more if possible.

Breaking out

Pause new actives. Single mild salicylic step at night. If breakouts last more than 4 weeks, escalate to a real plan.

Tight, red, reactive

Barrier-repair mode: cut all actives for 7–10 days. Cleanser + ceramide moisturizer + SPF only. Reintroduce slowly.

Pigmented or uneven

Vitamin C AM, retinoid PM, SPF religiously. Add a professional brightening peel every 4–6 weeks.

When to bring in a professional

  • You’ve given an OTC routine 8 weeks and your skin hasn’t shifted.
  • You have deep, painful, or scarring acne.
  • You want predictable, planned results for an event.
  • You’re ready for the long-term skin asset, not the next quick fix.
